Singer–Songwriter · Composer · Producer

Jihae Kimm

Jihae Kimm is a singer–songwriter, composer, and producer based in Seoul and Boston. Her work includes songs featured in films and Netflix original series, and she has directed music for a short film by acclaimed director Min Kyu-dong. She is currently attending Berklee College of Music on a full presidential scholarship.
